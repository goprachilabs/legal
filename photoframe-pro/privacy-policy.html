Privacy Policy

PhotoFrame Pro · Effective date: 9 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how GO PRACHI LABS (“we”, “us”, or “our”) handles information in connection with the mobile application PhotoFrame Pro (package name natureframes.frames.climatephoto.photo.photoframes.naturephotoframes) (the “App”). Your privacy matters to us. Please read this policy carefully. By using the App, you agree to the practices described here.

In plain terms: the App lets you frame, edit, collage, and print your photos, turn them into short motion-greeting videos, frame many photos at once, and set reminders for birthdays and other occasions. The photos you choose are processed on your device and are not uploaded to our own servers. We use trusted Google services for ads, analytics, crash reporting and notifications, and we download frame designs from our content delivery network.

1. Information we collect and how we use it

Photos and media you select

To frame, edit, crop, filter, collage, animate, batch-process, or print your pictures, the App needs access to the images you choose. On Android 13 and above this uses the READ_MEDIA_IMAGES permission (or per-photo selected access), and on older versions the legacy storage-read permission; you can also use the system Photo Picker, which requires no permission at all. The images you select are processed on your device. Finished creations you save are written back to your device’s gallery (Pictures, Movies, and Documents folders). We do not upload your photos to our own servers.

Camera (optional)

If you choose the “take a photo” option, the App launches your device’s camera app to capture a picture, which is then returned to the App and processed on your device. The App does not access the camera directly and does not record or upload video.

On-device background removal

The background-removal feature uses Google ML Kit Subject Segmentation, which runs entirely on your device to separate a subject from its background. Your images are not sent off the device for this feature.

Occasion reminders

If you use the Occasion Planner, the names, dates, and details you enter for birthdays, anniversaries, and festivals are stored on your device and used only to schedule local reminder notifications. This information stays on your device.

Device and usage information

We use Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Crashlytics to understand how the App is used and to diagnose crashes. This may include app events (such as features opened or frames saved), device model, operating-system version, app version, language, and general region/country, and crash diagnostics. This helps us fix bugs and improve the App.

Advertising information

The App shows ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may access your device’s advertising identifier and related data to serve and measure ads, including personalized ads where permitted. Some premium frames can be unlocked by watching a rewarded ad; this does not involve any payment. See Section 4 for your ad choices.

Notifications

If you enable notifications, the App uses Firebase Cloud Messaging (which generates a device push token) to deliver optional updates, in addition to the local occasion reminders described above. You can turn notifications off at any time in your device settings.

Frame catalog and assets

The App downloads frame designs and related assets from our content delivery network (Cloudflare). Fetching these assets does not require any personal information from you; as with any internet request, standard technical information such as your IP address is handled by the network provider to deliver the content. We also use Firebase Remote Config to manage app settings and the available frame catalog.

Information you choose to provide

If you email us (for support or feedback), we receive the email address and message content you send, and use it only to respond to you.

2. Content you create

Frames, collages, videos, PDFs, saved projects, custom frames, and occasion entries you create stay on your device (in your gallery or in the App’s storage) until you delete them or uninstall the App. We do not have access to this content.
