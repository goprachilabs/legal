Delete Your Data

Compass 360 Pro by GO PRACHI LABS · Effective date: 10 September 2026

Compass 360 Pro (package name compass.flashlight.speedometer) does not use accounts or sign-in, and does not store your personal data on our servers — everything the app remembers about you lives only on your own device. Because of that, you don’t need to contact us to delete your data: you can do it yourself, instantly, in two ways.

Option 1 — Delete your data inside the app

  1. Open Compass 360 Pro.
  2. Go to Settings (the gear icon on the Home screen).
  3. Scroll to the bottom of Settings and tap Delete my data (shown in red).
  4. Confirm by tapping Delete everything.

This erases the data immediately and permanently — there is no waiting period, and nothing is queued or retained afterward, since none of it was ever sent to us in the first place.

Option 2 — Uninstall the app

Uninstalling Compass 360 Pro from your device removes all of the same local data immediately, for the same reason: none of it exists anywhere except on your device.

What gets deleted

  • Trip history, including automatically detected trips (Automatic Trip & Mileage Log)
  • Saved locations (favorites/pins) and Compass waypoints
  • Projects, including Trail Retrace routes, Floor Plan images, Checklist entries, and project Timeline history
  • The business/brand logo used for PDF report stamps
  • Cached location and address data used to speed up the map, weather, and altimeter features

What is not deleted

  • Your Pro purchase. This is verified by Google Play, not stored by us — if you reinstall the app, tapping Restore Purchase in the Go Pro screen brings it back.
  • App settings such as language, theme, and measurement units.
  • Photos and videos already saved to your device’s own Gallery app. Deleting a project removes it from Compass 360 Pro but, consistent with how the app already behaves when you delete a single project, never touches the original photos or videos in your Gallery.
Retention period: none. Because none of the data above is transmitted to or stored on our servers, there is nothing left to retain once you delete it from your device or uninstall the app.
