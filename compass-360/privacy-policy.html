Privacy Policy

Compass 360 Pro · Effective date: 27 July 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how GO PRACHI LABS (“we”, “us”, or “our”) handles information in connection with the mobile application Compass 360 Pro (package name compass.flashlight.speedometer) (the “App”). Your privacy matters to us. Please read this policy carefully. By using the App, you agree to the practices described here.

In plain terms: the App shows a compass, speedometer, altimeter, your location on a map, area measurements, weather, world clock, a flashlight, a bubble level and an AR tape measure. Your location and sensor data are processed on your device and are not uploaded to our own servers. We use trusted Google services for maps, ads, analytics, crash reporting, notifications and purchases, and third-party providers for weather and elevation data, as described below.

1. Information we collect and how we use it

Location information (precise and approximate)

With your permission, the App accesses your device’s GPS and network-based location to provide its core features: showing your position and coordinates on a map, calculating compass heading and magnetic declination, measuring speed and trips (Speedometer), measuring altitude (Altimeter), calculating area and distances (Area Calculator), finding the Qibla direction, and displaying sun/moon times and weather for your location. Location is processed on your device. It may be sent to the third-party services listed in Section 3 (for example, to render maps, retrieve weather conditions, or look up elevation). We do not store your location on our own servers.

Camera

With your permission, the App uses your device camera for the AR Tape Measure (which uses Google ARCore), the optional camera background in the Bubble Level, and the camera-overlay Compass. The Flashlight feature controls your camera’s flash (torch) hardware. The camera preview is processed on your device in real time; the App does not capture, store, or upload your photos or video.

Device and usage information

We use Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Crashlytics to understand how the App is used and to diagnose crashes. This may include app events (such as features opened), device model, operating-system version, app version, language, and general region/country, and crash diagnostics. This helps us fix bugs and improve the App.

Advertising information

The free version of the App shows ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may access your device’s advertising identifier and related data to serve and measure ads, including personalized ads where permitted. See Section 4 for your ad choices.

Notifications

If you enable notifications, the App uses Firebase Cloud Messaging (which generates a device push token) to deliver optional reminders and updates. You can turn notifications off at any time in your device settings.

Purchases

If you buy the optional one-time “Pro” upgrade (which removes ads and unlocks premium compass themes), the transaction is processed by Google Play Billing. We receive confirmation that a purchase was made so we can unlock Pro features and restore them for you. We never see or store your payment card or billing details — those are handled entirely by Google.

Information you choose to provide

If you email us (for support or feedback), we receive the email address and message content you send, and use it only to respond to you.

2. Sensors

The App reads device motion and environment sensors — the accelerometer and magnetometer (for the compass, bubble level, and tilt/inclinometer features) and the barometric pressure sensor where available (for the Altimeter). This sensor data is used on your device in real time and is not personal information about you.

4. Ads and your choices

Ads are served by Google AdMob. You can control personalized advertising on your device:

  • Android: Settings → Google → Ads → opt out of / reset your advertising ID.
  • Removing ads entirely: purchase the one-time Pro upgrade in the App.

5. How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. Information is shared only with the service providers listed in Section 3 so they can perform their functions (for example, showing maps or ads), and where required by law, legal process, or to protect the rights, safety, or property of our users, the public, or us.

6. Data retention

Settings, saved locations, and trip history created by the App stay on your device until you delete them or uninstall the App. Data handled by the third-party services in Section 3 is retained according to their respective policies.

8. Children’s privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us information, please contact us and we will delete it.
