Privacy Policy

GPS Map Camera & Compass  ·  Effective date: 9 July 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how GO PRACHI LABS (“we”, “us”, or “our”) handles information in connection with the mobile application GPS Map Camera & Compass (package name compass.photo.camera.map.gps.gpsmapcamera_compass) (the “App”). Your privacy matters to us. Please read this policy carefully. By using the App, you agree to the practices described here.

In plain terms: the App stamps your photos with your location, shows a compass and maps, and displays sun/moon and weather details. Your photos and location are processed on your device and are not uploaded to our own servers. We use trusted Google services for maps, ads, analytics, crash reporting, notifications, and purchases, as described below.

1. Information we collect and how we use it

Location information (precise and approximate)

With your permission, the App accesses your device’s GPS and network-based location to provide its core features: geotagging your photos and videos (embedding coordinates and address into the image), showing your position on a map, calculating compass heading, magnetic declination, and sun/moon and weather details for your location. Location is processed on your device and embedded into the media you capture. It may be sent to the third-party services listed in Section 3 (for example, to render maps or retrieve weather conditions). We do not store your location on our own servers.

Camera, photos, and media

With your permission, the App uses your camera to take photos and record videos, and reads/writes media so it can save your captures to your gallery and display them in the in-app album. Your photos and videos remain on your device unless you choose to share or export them. We do not upload or retain your photos or videos.

Device and usage information

We use Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Crashlytics to understand how the App is used and to diagnose crashes. This may include app events (such as features opened), device model, operating-system version, app version, language, and general region/country, and crash diagnostics. This helps us fix bugs and improve the App.

Advertising information

The free version of the App shows ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may access your device’s advertising identifier and related data to serve and measure ads, including personalized ads where permitted. See Section 4 for your ad choices.

Notifications

If you enable notifications, the App uses Firebase Cloud Messaging (which generates a device push token) to deliver optional reminders such as golden-hour alerts. You can turn notifications off at any time in your device settings.

Purchases

If you buy the optional one-time “Remove Ads / Pro” upgrade, the transaction is processed by Google Play Billing. We receive confirmation that a purchase was made so we can unlock Pro features and restore them for you. We never see or store your payment card or billing details — those are handled entirely by Google.

Information you choose to provide

If you email us (for support or feedback), we receive the email address and message content you send, and use it only to respond to you.

2. Sensors

The App reads device motion sensors (accelerometer and magnetometer) to power the compass, level, and tilt features. This sensor data is used on your device in real time and is not personal information about you.

3. Third-party services

The App relies on the following third-party services, each governed by its own privacy policy:

  • Google Play Services — policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google Maps Platform — policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google AdMob (advertising) — support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543
  • Google Analytics for Firebase & Firebase Crashlytics — firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (notifications) — firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • Google Play Billing (purchases) — policies.google.com/privacy
  • A third-party weather data provider may be queried using your location to display current conditions.

4. Ads and your choices

Ads are served by Google AdMob. You can control personalized advertising on your device:

  • Android: Settings → Google → Ads → opt out of / reset your advertising ID.
  • Removing ads entirely: purchase the one-time Pro upgrade in the App.

5. How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. Information is shared only with the service providers listed in Section 3 so they can perform their functions (for example, showing maps or ads), and where required by law, legal process, or to protect the rights, safety, or property of our users, the public, or us.

6. Data retention

Photos, videos, and app settings created by the App stay on your device until you delete them or uninstall the App. Data handled by the third-party services in Section 3 is retained according to their respective policies.

7. Security

8. Children’s privacy
